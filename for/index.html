Who it’s for

Every team has something to say. Most say it in one language.

Octavia takes the video and audio you already publish and returns it in 60+ languages — dubbed, subtitled and transcribed, and reviewed before anything ships. What changes from team to team is what has to survive the translation. Find yours below.

Faith & Ministries

NGOs & Humanitarian
